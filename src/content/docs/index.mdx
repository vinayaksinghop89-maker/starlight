## Meet AURA

AURA is a virtual mobile robot with a simple mission: find the red target, plan a
route around obstacles, and tag it before time runs out. The 3D arena runs in
your browser—no robot or special hardware needed.

<CardGrid stagger>
	<Card title="Autonomous navigation" icon="rocket">
		AURA uses A* pathfinding to navigate around the arena's obstacles.
	</Card>
	<Card title="A game of tag" icon="open-book">
		The red target flees at the same speed. If AURA can't catch it in 60 seconds,
		the target chases and you steer AURA with WASD or the arrow keys.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Explore in 3D" icon="open-book">
		Drag to orbit the camera and scroll to zoom in or out while the simulation runs.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
